## Notable observation

In the Q2/LIRA column, p1 = 1.000x exactly, confirming that the
catastrophic 0.001x worst case is confined to well under 1% of the 807
tasks, with performance already back at parity by the 1st percentile --
i.e., not evidence of a broadly-skewed distribution.
